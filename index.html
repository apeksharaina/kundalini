What is Kundalini

Yoga for your energy, not just your body

In yogic tradition, Kundalini is the energy that rests at the base of the spine. Kundalini Yoga works with it through kriyas: set sequences of posture, breath, mantra and stillness, to help you feel calmer, more focused and more connected to yourself. No flexibility or experience needed.
